How it works

Pull a card. Talk it through. Coach each other.

  1. 1
    Pull a cardRead the scenario out loud. Every one is a real situation managers run into.
  2. 2
    Share what you'd doEach person says how they'd handle it, in their own words. The differences are the point.
  3. 3
    Flip it and coachThe back offers approaches to consider. Compare notes and sharpen how you'd respond.

About 10 minutes. Fits the top of any team meeting.
